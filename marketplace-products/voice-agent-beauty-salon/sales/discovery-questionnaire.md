# Beauty salon discovery questionnaire

## Business

- Locations, opening hours, languages.
- Monthly incoming calls and missed-call estimate.
- Average booking value and no-show rate.
- Existing phone, calendar, CRM and messaging tools.

## Booking

- Services, duration, price, staff eligibility.
- Buffers, deposits, cancellation and late-arrival policies.
- Same-day booking rules and exceptions.

## Risk

- Recording consent and retention.
- Sensitive services or medical-adjacent questions.
- Human escalation owner and response hours.

## Acceptance

- Target booking success rate.
- Maximum tolerated error rate.
- Pilot number, duration and test volume.
