# Proposal scope template

## Outcome

Deploy voice booking assistant for one salon location and one phone number.

## Included

- Discovery and service catalog mapping.
- Provider and n8n configuration.
- Google Calendar connection.
- Booking, cancellation and human handoff flows.
- 12 acceptance tests.
- Team handoff and 14-day stabilization.

## Excluded

- Telephony/provider usage fees.
- Custom CRM development beyond documented webhook.
- Medical advice, payments by phone, outbound campaigns.
- Legal review of recording/consent language.

## Acceptance

12/12 tests pass; zero duplicate bookings; rollback demonstrated; client approves service catalog and consent notice.

## Commercial model example

Setup fee + monthly monitoring/support. Quote from measured call volume and integration complexity; do not promise savings before baseline exists.
